Privacy Policy — Parental Control App

Last updated: October 29, 2026

📋 Introduction

Welcome to KidFix – Parental Control ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application.

KidFix is designed exclusively for parents and legal guardians to monitor and protect their children's devices. By using this app, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

⚠️ This app is intended for use only by parents/guardians on devices they own or have legal authority to monitor. Unauthorized use is strictly prohibited.

📊 Information We Collect

KidFix collects the following types of information to provide parental monitoring features:

Data Type Purpose Stored?
Google Account Info (email, name) Parent authentication via Google Sign-In Encrypted
Device Information Identify and pair child devices Secure
Camera / Photos Live Camera & Ghost Photo features Not stored
Microphone / Audio Live Audio monitoring feature Not stored
Screen Content Live Screen Viewer feature Not stored
Contacts View child's contact list Not stored
Call History / SMS Monitor child's call & message history Not stored
Location Data Device overview & safety features Not stored

🔧 How We Use Information

We use collected information only for the following purposes:

  • Authenticate the parent/guardian account via Google Sign-In
  • Enable secure pairing between parent and child devices
  • Provide real-time monitoring features (camera, audio, screen)
  • Display child device activity (calls, messages, contacts)
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Send important app notifications and updates

✅ We do NOT sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

🔐 Sensitive Permissions

KidFix requires the following sensitive permissions on the child's device:

  • Camera — For Live Camera and Ghost Photo features
  • Microphone — For Live Audio monitoring
  • Read Contacts — To display child's contact list to parent
  • Read Call Log — To display call history to parent
  • Read SMS — To display message history to parent
  • Screen Capture — For Live Screen Viewer feature
  • Internet — To transmit data between parent and child devices

⚠️ These permissions must only be granted on devices owned by you or under your legal guardianship. All monitoring is done transparently with device pairing consent.

🗄️ Data Storage & Security

KidFix uses Firebase (Google) for secure backend services. All data transmitted between devices is encrypted using industry-standard protocols.

  • Live monitoring data (camera, audio, screen) is streamed in real-time and not stored on our servers
  • Account data is stored securely in Firebase with encryption
  • We implement technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access
  • Data is automatically deleted when you delete your account

🌐 Third-Party Services

KidFix uses the following trusted third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In — For secure parent authentication
  • Firebase (Google) — For real-time database, authentication, and cloud functions
  • Google Play Services — For app distribution and updates

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy Policy

👶 Children's Privacy

KidFix is designed to protect children's safety, not to collect their personal data. The app is operated by parents/guardians and no data from child devices is used for any commercial purpose.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. All data collected from child devices is accessible only to the paired parent account.

⚖️ Your Rights

As a user of KidFix, you have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of your stored data
  • Delete — Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Correct — Update or correct your account information
  • Withdraw Consent — Uninstall the app and revoke permissions at any time
  • Data Portability — Request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.
